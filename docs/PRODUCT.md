# IMarket product specification

## Differentiators
- Standalone instant shopping (later, with real provider-backed checkout)
- Pinduoduo-inspired supplier-funded group buying with documented eligibility and transparent campaign expiry
- CDcare-inspired optional savings/prepayments and eventually regulated early-delivery installment options
- Seller-led campaigns, local delivery and dispute management

## Safety definitions
A **group discount** is a seller-funded discount with clear price, thresholds, exclusions and cancellation policy. It is not a pyramid, referral investment or guaranteed reward. **Prepayment** means paying toward goods with conditions agreed in advance; credit/financing is separately assessed and not presumed lawful. Group failure must lead to a clear, timely reversal/refund when real payments are later enabled.

## Key personas
1. Buyer: browses products and checks genuine seller discounts; can track group progress.
2. Seller: verified business offering inventory-backed discounted campaigns.
3. Operator: moderates listings, investigates fraud and refunds, manages legal holds.

## MVP measures (proposed, not achieved)
- Fulfilment reliability, group completion, conversion, refund time, verified product accuracy.
- No financial ROI or revenue guarantees to buyers or merchants.

## Pilot
Start with trusted sellers and a narrow catalog. Kaduna can be a proposed first pilot location; no live launch has occurred.
